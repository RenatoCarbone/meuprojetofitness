💡 2. What is MiPlanFit?

MiPlanFit is a web-based nutrition and fitness planning software built specifically for the European and Spanish-speaking market. It utilizes scientifically validated anthropometric equations (such as Mifflin-St Jeor) to calculate exact caloric needs (BMR/TDEE) and BMI metrics.

Based on individual user parameters (weight, height, age, activity level, and goals), the system generates a personalized 30-day routine featuring structured meal plans, food substitution options, categorized grocery shopping lists, and daily habit tracking.
